## Design conventions

- **Title slide (1):** dark navy background, white headline, format/duration footer
- **Code-along cue slides (11, 20):** dark navy background, green "🟢 CODE-ALONG" pill, timer card at the bottom
- **Discussion cue slide (15):** dark navy background, gold "💬 DISCUSSION" pill (visual cue to discuss rather than code)
- **Bridge / closing slides (21, 22):** dark or accent navy with quoted text card
- **Content slides:** white background, thin teal top bar, NAVY title 32pt bold, slide-number footer
- **Code blocks:** Consolas on midnight `#21295C` background
- **Stats:** large numbers in Trebuchet MS 44-72pt
- **Comparisons:** two-column or three-column equal-width boxes
